Our Story

Designed for real life, made to last

Urban Threads started with a simple idea: clothing should feel as good as it looks. Every piece is developed with breathable, durable fabrics and finished with the kind of detail you notice a year in — not just on day one.

We work directly with a small group of mills and makers, keeping runs limited so every drop stays considered rather than disposable.
